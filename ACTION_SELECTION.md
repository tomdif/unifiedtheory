W = (2,-4,2) (the 2D Benincasa-Dowker Einstein-Hilbert
coefficients).  Both BD systems showed everywhere-feasibility
(0 infeasible parents to depth 79) - the conjecture: DEEP
FEASIBILITY OF BI-NORMALIZED GROWTH SELECTS THE BD ACTION, i.e.
Einstein's action forced by quantum consistency.  If true: the
ultimate closure - measure, phase, AND action all derived.

## The test (pre-registered readings i/ii/iii in
## action_selection.py)

Scan of 140 integer weight systems (w0 in 1..4, w1 in -6..0,
w2 in 0..4), 15-point phase grid, maxent law, prefilter + 4 paths
to depth 12; depth-16 stress test of the top ten.

## Verdict: REFUTED as stated (reading ii/iii)

  - 89/140 systems achieve FULL survival with nondegenerate laws
    (support > 0.3); 43 dead, 8 partial.  Deep feasibility is a
    BROAD BASIN (~2/3 of sampled weight space), not a razor edge.
  - BD (2,-4,2) ranks 4th by support (0.941) - comfortably inside
    the basin, in no way singled out.
  - Depth-16 stress: 9/10 top survivors hold (BD included);
    notably the TOP-ranked system (2,-2,4) collapsed at depth 16 -
    feasibility horizons exist, but they do not carve out BD at
    accessible depth.
  QUANTUM CONSISTENCY DOES NOT SELECT THE ACTION.
