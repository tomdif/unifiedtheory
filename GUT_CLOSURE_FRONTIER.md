# What remains before this is a physical unified theory

Date: 2026-09-09

**The repository does not establish a complete physical GUT, a derivation
of GR, or a recovered Standard Model quantum field theory.** Here “GUT”
refers to the broader unified-theory program in this repository, including
gravity. The narrower conventional meaning of grand unification would
still require physical gauge and matter dynamics beyond finite algebraic
bookkeeping.
