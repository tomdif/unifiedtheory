A(C)e^{i(S-1)phi} (the coboundary property), so with covariant
final-class identification the decoherence functional between
stage-n events at horizon T telescopes to

nonnegative: EVENT-LEVEL INTERFERENCE IN THIS THEORY IS ALWAYS
CONSTRUCTIVE, the quantum measure is everywhere superadditive, and
decoherence can only mean weighted future-orthogonality of sectors.
