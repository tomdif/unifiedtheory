# Causal record dynamics

## Result

The sharp native effect used by the causal resolution law is no longer an
independent probability postulate.  It follows from a recorded microscopic
dynamics with two properties:

1. refinement is lossless, so its recorded Stinespring map `V` is an
   isometry;
2. the identity of the causal child is transported through refinement:

   `Q_c V = V P_c`.

Here `P_c` restricts the incoming native-child carrier to child `c`, while
`Q_c` reads the same child in the newly created record.  Lean proves

```text
V† V = I  and  Q_c V = V P_c
  ==> K_c† K_c = P_c.
```

If the coherent record counit also recovers the unresolved parent,

```text
E V = I,
```

then the operators themselves are forced:

```text
K_c = P_c = |c><c|.
```

Thus the native resolution law is derived from lossless refinement,
coherent recovery, and conservation of causal-record identity.  No separate
Born-effect, matrix-support, locality, or nondemolition axiom enters the
microscopic theorem.

The quantum outcome is also explicit. For every density matrix `rho`,

```text
Pr(c | rho) = Re Tr(rho K_c† K_c) = Re rho[c,c].
```

In particular, a system prepared in native child `d` yields `c=d` with
probability one and every `c≠d` with probability zero. This outcome theorem is
derived from isometry and causal-record transport through the sharp effect;
it is not separately assumed.

## Further deductions

The derived state has four exact consequences.

- **Repeatability:** `K_c K_c = K_c`.
- **Native superselection:** the reduced channel is exactly dephasing in the
  physical-child basis.
- **Protected algebra:** a state or observable is fixed exactly when all its
  cross-child entries vanish.  The protected finite algebra is therefore the
  classical algebra generated by the native child projectors.
- **Complementarity boundary:** the two Pauli-`Y` orientation characters are
  identified by native-record resolution if placed in the same carrier.
  Chirality must occupy a separate protected algebra, be diagonalized in a

## Independence theorem

The alignment equation is indispensable. The repository contains an exact
two-outcome counterexample formed by the positive and negative orientation
projectors. Its recorded refinement satisfies

```text

```

but fails `Q_c V = V P_c`; equivalently, `K_c† K_c` is not the projector
onto native child `c`. Consequently, losslessness, exact coherent parent
recovery, and label covariance do not determine which sharp basis nature
records. This is a constructive independence proof, not merely the absence
of a derivation.
