1. Precise class and geometry
Fix alpha>0,beta>0,a>0, |b|<a and alpha(a^2-b^2)>8 beta b^2.
Fix finitely many real widths w_i>=1 and arbitrary complex d_i=c_i w_i.
Fix one common REAL neutral source B and the neutral polynomial P=1.
These labels are held fixed under all clock/geometry derivatives.
Set r=a+ib, p=alpha r^2/2, A=a^2-b^2, R=a^2+b^2, eta=2b.
F(z)=sum_i d_i exp(-w_i z), z=p(u^2+v^2)+conj(p)Y, Y>0.
Use the original quartic W(t)=1+t^2/2+t^4/4. Up to the constant pi/4,
which cancels from every displayed differential inequality, the ORIGINAL
full energy is
 E(a,b,L)=int du dv dY dx W(x+Lv)|F(z)|^2
            exp(-2 beta x^2+2 B x+8 beta b^2 v^2).
This is exactly |exp(-beta(x+i2bv)^2+B(x+i2bv))|^2.
All coherent width cross terms remain. This is not a single-width theorem.
The strict displayed floor gives a local common Gaussian majorant for the
energy and its first geometry derivatives. Constants in that majorant may
depend on the finite packet; the final response constant will not.

2. Actual even charged moments and the matrix lower bound
I_l=int du dv dY v^(2l)|F(p(u^2+v^2)+conj(p)Y)|^2.
Let S_ij=p w_i+conj(p)w_j, T_ij=conj(S_ij), K_l=S_ij^(-l-1)T_ij^-1.
With c_l=pi (2l)!/(4^l l!), I_l=c_l sum_ij d_i conj(d_j) K_l(i,j).
The coefficient order follows by writing F times conj(F). Equivalently
this is the standard Hermitian quadratic form with coefficient conj(d).
The exact Gaussian/Laplace integral produces c_l, not an unspecified
positive normalization. Recursively c_0=pi and c_(l+1)=(2l+1)c_l/2.
Every K_l is positive semidefinite, by the positive inverse-Laplace Gram
kernel S^-1 and the Schur product theorem; T^-1 is equally positive.

No division by b is required in the following identity:
 b S_b=u S+v0 T, b T_b=u T+v0 S,
 u=1/2-b^2/A, v0=-R/(2A).
Consequently
 b (K_l)_b=-(l+2)u K_l
             -v0[(l+1)S^(-l-2)+S^(-l)T^-2].
Thus the following exact residual is positive semidefinite:
 b (K_l)_b+(l+2)K_l/2
   = (l+2)b^2/A K_l
     +R/(2A)[(l+1)S^(-l-2)+S^(-l)T^-2] >=0.
For l=0 the last term is T^-2; no negative power or lost boundary term.
At b=0 the formula is nonsingular. It gives a lower bound, not exact
positivity of (K_l)_b. All statements include coherent finite width sums.
Hence b (I_l)_b >= -(l+2) I_l/2 for every natural l.

3. Positive quartic decomposition
Let M_j=int_R x^j exp(-2 beta x^2+2Bx)dx, j=0,2,4. These are finite,
nonnegative, with M_0>0. Since |F(z)|^2 is even in v, all odd-v terms in
W(x+Lv) vanish after v integration, including when B is not zero.
Define
 A_0=M_0+M_2/2+M_4/4,
 A_1=L^2(M_0/2+3 M_2/2), A_2=L^4 M_0/4.
Every A_m is nonnegative. Exact Tonelli expansion of the nonnegative
exp(8 beta b^2 v^2) gives
 E=sum_(m=0)^2 A_m sum_(n>=0) (8 beta)^n/n! b^(2n) I_(m+n).
Odd-v cancellation is performed in the already absolutely integrable
polynomial integral before applying Tonelli. It is not a termwise sign
assumption on the coherent charged expansion.

For b>0, every summand of b^2 E is nondecreasing as b increases within
0<b<a (with the full energy finite whenever the strict floor holds):
 b d_b[b^(2n+2) I_(m+n)]
 >= [2n+2-(m+n+2)/2] b^(2n+2) I_(m+n)
 = (3n+2-m)/2 b^(2n+2) I_(m+n) >=0, since m<=2.
First prove this finite-increment monotonicity for each differentiable
moment, then sum the nonnegative inequalities. This avoids any exchange
of differentiation with the infinite series. The already justified
actual derivative of E therefore gives
                         b E_b >= -2 E.
For b<0, either repeat the argument in t=-b, or conjugate the fixed width
amplitudes and reflect b. At b=0 the claimed inequality is immediate.
Also A_m is proportional to L^(2m), so for L>0,
                         0 <= L E_L <= 4 E.
This is again an actual finite polynomial identity after the v parity
cancellation, not an independent-jet relaxation.

4. Actual-clock conversion (existing real-dilation identity)
Let fraction=t>=0, q=delta/a, h=adot/a, chi=h/q in the actual nonzero-q
case. The frozen actual-clock identities/bounds give
 E_dot=-4h E-q[b E_b+3chi E_L],
 0<=q<=20t, |h|<=8t b^2, b^2<=1/2, |chi|<=1, L>=1/2.
Thus b E_b+3chi E_L >= -2E-3*(4/L)E >=-26E,
 E_dot <= (16+20*26)t E =536t E.
At the q=0 clock endpoint use the actual identity before dividing by q;
the original derivative vanishes there. At b=0 the actual real-dilation
cancellation is preserved; the coarse estimate does not need equality.
The bound is independent of the number/values of charged widths, charged
amplitudes, common real B, beta and alpha within the stated physical floors.
It is restricted to one common real neutral source and P=1.
