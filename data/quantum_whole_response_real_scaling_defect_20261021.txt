2. Exact scaling, BEFORE integration against neutral jets

For every t>0, with k fixed, changing u,v,Y to u/t,v/t,Y/t^2 gives
  K_m(t*a,t*b;k)=t^(-m-4)*K_m(a,b;k).
Indeed p scales by t^2 and eta by t, so the neutral Fourier tilt eta*k*v is
unchanged; the spatial measure scales by t^-4. If L is also multiplied by t,
then nu scales by t. Every block of the full B matrix consequently satisfies

  B(t*a,t*b,t*L;k)=t^-4 B(a,b,L;k),
  a*B_a+b*B_b+L*B_L=-4B.                       (1)

This is an entrywise finite-matrix identity, not merely an integrated
cancellation that assumes independent neutral jets. It retains all cross
widths and the complete quartic form. It also uses no division by b or k.

3. Actual combined clock generator and its small defect

Write the actual complex velocity as rdot=delta+ell*r, with delta>0, and
Ldot=-2*adot. Let
  q=delta/a,
  h=adot/a=ell+q,
  chi=h/q.
Then bdot=(h-q)*b and Ldot=-2ha. Substituting (1) gives the exact identity

  Bdot=-4h B-q*[b B_b+3chi B_L].                (2)

Thus the entire order-one field drift has already canceled. In the frozen
normalized clock notation, with t=fraction,

  q=t*radius(r)/denominator(1-t,t,r),
  h=t*realNumerator(t,r)/(2*denominator(1-t,t,r)),
  chi=realNumerator(t,r)/(2*radius(r)).

The existing physical bounds imply
  0<q<=20t,
  |h|<=8t*b^2,
  |chi|<=2b^2/radius(r)<=2b^2,
  b^2<=1/2.
Only the ACTUAL chi is intended here; replacing it by arbitrary parameters
throughout this bounding interval is a stronger, unproved statement.

A sufficient state-independent estimate is therefore precisely

  integral jet(g)^* [b B_b+3chi B_L] jet(g) dk
      >= -C*integral jet(g)^*B jet(g) dk.       (3)

If (3) holds uniformly on the actual physical clock range and original finite
families, (2) yields Bdot energy <=(16+20C)*t*E_F. All external normalizations
multiply both sides. Formula (3) has NOT been established by this note.
It is the current exact combined defect target, not a separated v^2 cooling
condition or a statement about independent radial/angular blocks.

4. Exact b=0 cancellation at the matrix level

At b=0 the moments are v-even and K_m=0 for odd m. For even m,
  K_m(a,0;k)=a^(-m-4)*K_m(1,0;k),
and they are independent of k. With H_m=-partial_a K_m,
  H_m=(m+4)K_m/a.
Consequently the complete matrices satisfy
  a*B(H,nu)-L*B_L=4B,
or equivalently b B_b=0 at b=0. This statement is pointwise in k and already
retains every neutral jet cross term.

For the actual zero-imaginary clock one has a=1,b=0,h=0,chi=0,q=t, so the
entire matrix Bdot vanishes. Neither the nonzero real charged derivative nor
the nonzero order-one field term should be estimated separately there. The
width-floor cooling obstruction is fully consistent with this cancellation.

5. One further exact integrated null term; all original linkage preserved

The b derivative changes eta=2b and nu=L-i*eta. Separate the nu change while
holding the charged feature/tilt matrix fixed. In channel j its contribution
to b*dE_j/db is

  2j Re integral (-i*eta*v) h_(j-1)*conj(h_j) dk du dv dY.

At each fixed charged point, Plancherel identifies
  integral h_(j-1)*conj(h_j) dk
with a real constant multiple of
  integral (x+Lv)^(2j-1)|Phi(z,x+i*eta*v)|^2 dx.
Therefore this nu-only contribution is exactly zero after the genuine k
integration. This is a null term for the linked original derivatives, not a
pointwise-in-k matrix identity, and not a license to replace g,Dg,D^2g by
independent vectors.

The remainder of b B_b includes BOTH charged imaginary motion and neutral
tilt. If C_m denotes partial_b K_m with eta held fixed inside the tilt, then
  b*partial_b K_m = b*C_m-2eta*k*K_(m+1).
The pure tilt term is the derivative of exp(-2eta*k*v). At fixed charged
radius its actual degree-j angular polynomial obeys the analytic Laurent
support bound tiltDerivative >=-2j*channelEnergy. Summed over j<=2 this gives
an exact degree cost at most4 times E_F. The already frozen Lean angular
subestimate uses a larger absolute constant and is sufficient for that
separate fact.

Dropping the remaining charged imaginary term is not justified. Scalar j=0
imaginary monotonicity concerns the SUM of charged motion and tilt; it does
not assert the charged term alone is nonnegative for the weighted channels.
Similarly the O(b^2) coefficient of B_L in (3) does not bound B_L/E uniformly:
large neutral source/frequency/charged-width correlations may scale with b.
These two steps remain genuine analytic obstacles, not missing algebra.
