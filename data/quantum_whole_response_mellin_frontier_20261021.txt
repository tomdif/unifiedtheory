1. Exact hypotheses and original energy

Fix alpha,beta>0, r=a+i b with 0<b<a, A=a^2-b^2,
R=a^2+b^2, eta=2b, L=3-2a, p=alpha*r^2/2, rho=|p|,
theta=arg(p) in (0,pi/2). Assume the strict packet floor
  alpha*A > 8*beta*b^2.
The physical geometry has further restrictions, but the identities here need
only these. The b=0 boundary requires the combined limit described below.
Use arbitrary FINITE widths w_i>=1, coefficients d_i, complex neutral sources
B_i and complex polynomials P_i, all HELD FIXED in the clock derivative:
  Phi(z,zeta)=sum_i exp(-w_i z) f_i(zeta),
  f_i(zeta)=d_i exp(-beta*zeta^2+B_i*zeta)P_i(zeta).
In original notation d_i=c_i*w_i. There is no independent-jet relaxation.
Set W(F)=1+F^2/2+F^4/4. Omitting only the unchanged external pi/4, the original
energy is
  E=integral_{u,v,x real,Y>0} W(x+Lv)
       |Phi(p(u^2+v^2)+bar(p)Y,x+i eta v)|^2 du dv dx dY.
All width/source/polynomial cross terms are present.

2. Scope-limited affine and logarithmic flow obstructions

The exact fixed holomorphic image from the preceding note is
  X=Re(cX z), Y=Re(cY z), v=Im(zeta)/eta, H=X-v^2,
  cX=(1/A-i/(2ab))/alpha, cY=(1/A+i/(2ab))/alpha,
  D={Y>0,H>0}, density proportional H^(-1/2)W(Re zeta+Lv).
Write the actual clock velocity rdot=delta+ell*r, delta>0, ell real,
  p0=(3a^2-b^2)/(2aA), q0=R/(2aA).
At fixed holomorphic coordinates the boundary speeds are
  Ydot|Y=0=-delta*q0*X,
  Hdot|H=0=-delta*(p0*X+q0*Y).
A compensating holomorphic field must offset BOTH inward speeds.

Consider the weighted-affine class
  Vz=A0*z+B0*zeta^2+C0,
  Vzeta=E0*zeta+F0.
The same obstruction permits additional z*zeta terms in Vz and terms in
Vzeta that do not affect d(v^2) at v=0. On zeta=0, Y=0, z=pX with X large,
  Re(cY Vz)=-Im(A0)csc(2theta) X+O(1).
On zeta=0, H=X=0, z=bar(p)Y with Y large,
  Re(cX Vz)=+Im(A0)csc(2theta) Y+O(1).
Offsetting both boundary speeds requires simultaneously
  Im(A0)<=-delta*q0*sin(2theta),
  Im(A0)>=+delta*q0*sin(2theta),
which is impossible. This only excludes this stated class. A z^2 field is
outside the class; this is not a no-go theorem for nonlinear holomorphic flows.

For the suggested branch of wedge narrowing, take the principal log on
|arg z|<theta and lambda>0:
  Vz=-lambda*z*log z,
  Vzeta=-(lambda/2)*log z*zeta.
Let l=log|z|, phi=arg z, chi=Re zeta+Lv. Direct differentiation gives
  V(Y)=-lambda*l*Y+lambda*phi*Im(cY z),
  V(H)=-lambda*l*H+lambda*phi*(Im(cX z)+v*Re(zeta)/eta),
  V(chi)=-(lambda/2)*(l+(L/eta)*phi)*chi
          +(lambda/2)*phi*(L^2+eta^2)*v/eta,
  real divergence(V)=-3lambda*l-2lambda.
Thus the log-radius terms in V(H) cancel ON H=0 exactly, as hoped. However,
on Y=H=0, z=pv^2 and chi=0, one has
  V(H)=-lambda*theta*L*v^2/eta,
which is outward when L>0 (the physical range). Moreover at fixed chi with
W'(chi) nonzero and v unbounded, V(chi) contains an unbounded linear-v term,
whereas the displayed divergence is only logarithmic. Adding further terms
might repair this, but this natural log lift is not the required contraction.
Neither computation disproves the full response estimate. Both explain why
an inward charged wedge map or a bounded interior-density claim is insufficient.

3. Fourier kernel and nonnegative Mellin densities

Use f_i(x)=integral_R exp(i k x) g_i(k) dk and D=i*d/dk. Hence
integral|f|^2 dx=2pi integral|g|^2 dk. The actual jth field channel is
  exp(-eta*k*v) (D+nu*v)^j g_i(k),  nu=L-i eta,  j=0,1,2.
Let E_F=E/(2pi). These constant normalizations have no effect on response ratios.
For each pair of widths define
  S_ij=p*w_i+bar(p)*w_j, T_ij=bar(p)*w_i+p*w_j.
After the full u,v,Y integrations the vacuum kernel and v moments are
  K_ij(k)=pi/(S_ij*T_ij)*exp(eta^2*k^2/S_ij),
  V_m,ij(k)=(-1/(2eta))^m (d/dk)^m K_ij(k).
This follows by the actual Gaussian u,v integrals and Laplace Y integral.
Writing t=log(w_i)-log(w_j), mbar=(log(w_i)+log(w_j))/2 gives
  K_ij(k)=sum_{n>=0} k^(2n) exp(-(n+2)mbar) C_n(t),
  C_n(t)=D_n0*cosh(t/2+i theta)^(-(n+1))*cosh(t/2-i theta)^(-1),
  D_n0=pi*eta^(2n)/(n!*(2rho)^(n+2)).
All powers here are integer powers. Let
  rho_n(omega)=(1/(2pi)) integral_R C_n(t) exp(-i omega t) dt.
Then C_n(t)=integral exp(i omega t)rho_n(omega)domega and rho_n>=0.
An explicit nonnegative formula is as follows:
  g_v(omega)=|Gamma(v/2+i omega)|^2/(2pi*Gamma(v)),
  rho_n(omega)=pi*eta^(2n)/(n!*rho^(n+2))
    * integral_R exp(-2theta*sigma)g_(n+1)(sigma)
                 exp(2theta*(omega-sigma))g_1(omega-sigma) dsigma.
The elementary beta-integral transform of (2cosh(t/2))^(-v), followed by a
contour shift smaller than pi, proves this formula. Equivalently positivity
follows from the inverse-S Laplace Gram kernels and their Schur products.
For n=0 one can check the normalization directly:
  rho_0(omega)=pi/(2rho^2 sin(2theta))
                *sinh(2theta*omega)/sinh(pi*omega),
with its continuous value at omega=0.

4. The actual linked Mellin features

Define, for real k and complex omega,
  H_n(k,omega)=sum_i w_i^(-(n+2)/2+i omega) g_i(k),
  L_n(k,omega)=k^n H_n(k,omega),  n>=0.
They are entire finite exponential sums in omega, and
  H_(n+1)(k,omega)=H_n(k,omega+i/2),
  L_(n+1)(k,omega)=k L_n(k,omega+i/2).
Since log(w_i)>=0, an upward imaginary shift multiplies each width amplitude
by w_i^(-shift). These features are NEVER independent functions of n or omega.
The labels, the g_i, and every L_n are clock-independent.

For j=0,1,2 the exact raw channel is
  E_j=sum_n integral_{k,omega} rho_n(omega)
       sum_{l,m=0}^j binom(j,l)binom(j,m)
       d0^l bar(d0)^m (2n)_falling(l+m)
       A_l * conjugate(A_m),
  d0=-nu/(2eta),
  A_l=k^(n-l) sum_i w_i^(-(n+2)/2+i omega) D^(j-l)g_i(k).
A term with l+m>2n is zero. This raw formula, with canceled powers of k,
is the nonsingular interpretation at k=0. E_F=E_0+E_1/2+E_2/4.

All exchanges can be justified for this original finite packet class: each
g_i and its finitely many derivatives is a polynomial times a Gaussian of
modulus bounded by const*(1+|k|)^M exp(-k^2/(4beta)+C|k|). Absolute summation
of the kernel/moment series is bounded by a polynomial times
exp(eta^2*k^2/(alpha*A)); the strict floor makes its product with two such
Gaussians integrable. In Mellin variables one can use
  integral rho_n=C_n(0)=pi*eta^(2n)/(n!*(alpha*A)^(n+2))
and the finite width floor. This also handles polynomial n factors. The same
bounds hold in a small parameter neighborhood, justifying fixed-label clock
differentiation. These are analytic justifications, not new Lean proofs.

5. Exact completed differential forms, including the quartic LDU

Set s=L/eta and define A_n=D-n*s/k on k!=0. For a linked L=L_n set
  q0_n[L]=|L|^2,
  q1_n,s[L]=|A_n L|^2 - n*(1+s^2)/(2k^2)*|L|^2,
  q2_n,s[L]=|A_n^2 L-n*(1+s^2)/(2k^2)*L|^2
       -2n*(1+s^2)/k^2*|A_n L+(s-i)/(2k)*L|^2
       +n*(2n-1)*(1+s^2)^2/(4k^4)*|L|^2,
  Q_n,s=q0_n+q1_n,s/2+q2_n,s/4.
Then exactly
  E_j=sum_n integral rho_n qj_n,s[L_n],
  E_F=sum_n integral rho_n Q_n,s[L_n].
The imaginary neutral shift cancels to the REAL connection ns/k because
  i+nu/eta=s.

For verification of the quartic formula, write N=2n and
  A0=D^2 L-2i*n*D L/k-n(n+1)*L/k^2,
  A1=(D L-i*n*L/k)/k,
  A2=L/k^2.
The falling-moment Hankel form has the exact LDU
  |A0+2d0*N*A1+d0^2*N(N-1)*A2|^2
  -N*|2d0*A1+2d0^2*(N-1)*A2|^2
  +2N(N-1)|d0|^4 |A2|^2.
Its first square is A_n^2 L-n(1+s^2)L/(2k^2); its middle expression is
  -(s-i)/k*(A_n L+(s-i)L/(2k)).
All three polynomial identities were independently expanded exactly with
SymPy (zero residuals); no floating numerical response test was involved.

The completed pieces MUST be combined before taking k->0 or integrating.
At n=1, j=2 individual displayed squares have k^(-2) singularities which
cancel in their sum. At n=0, q1=|DL_0|^2 and q2=|D^2L_0|^2, with all terms
carrying n defined as zero, not as a separately evaluated singular product.
At b->0 one likewise cannot discard n>=1 merely because rho_n has eta^(2n):
s=L/eta and the moment factors compensate. The raw combined expression has
the correct finite limit; terms n<=2 can survive in the quartic channel.

Individual n forms are not positive. Already the j=1 falling-moment matrix
at n=1 is [[1,2],[2,2]], of determinant -2 (after harmless nonzero scalar
congruence). This is NOT a realizable counterexample: the features satisfy
the exact width/Hardy linkage, and their full sum is the original positive
energy.

6. Half-shift recurrence, contour validity, and the boundary mode

The cosh relation gives the EXACT recurrence
  exp(i theta)rho_(n+1)(omega+i/2)
  +exp(-i theta)rho_(n+1)(omega-i/2)
      =2 q_n rho_n(omega),
  q_n=eta^2/(2rho*(n+1)).
The density rho_n is holomorphic in |Im omega|<(n+2)/2. Uniformly in a smaller
closed substrip it decays faster than exp(-sigma*|Re omega|) for every
sigma<pi-2theta, by shifting the defining t contour. Finite width sums are
bounded on each horizontal omega strip. Therefore the half-shift contour
moves used below are justified and cross NO poles.

For a finite exponential sum H, write H^*(z)=conjugate(H(conjugate z)).
Integrating the recurrence against H_n(omega)H_n^*(omega), and shifting its
two terms in opposite directions, gives the precise identity
  q_n integral rho_n |H_n|^2
   =Re[exp(i theta) integral rho_(n+1)
          H_(n-1)(omega)*conjugate(H_(n+1)(omega))].
Here H_(-1)=sum_i w_i^(-1/2+i omega)g_i is explicitly defined if n=0.
For n>=1 this may also be written with L_(n-1),L_(n+1) after restoring k
powers. The k powers cancel in that pairing. At n=0 the H_(-1) term is a
real boundary feature absent from the original nonnegative-index energy;
it cannot simply be dropped.

In particular
  |H_n(omega+i/2)|^2=H_n(omega+i/2)H_n^*(omega-i/2).
A common contour shift preserves the separation i between these arguments.
It does NOT replace this expression by |H_n(omega)|^2. This is the reason
naive adjacent-square telescoping is invalid.

The nearest meromorphic poles of rho_n occur at omega=+i(n+2)/2 and its
negative, from the two tails of C_n(t). With
  A_n^+=pi*eta^(2n)/(n!*rho^(n+2))*exp(-i*n*theta),
the residue at the upper pole is A_n^+/(2pi*i); the lower residue is
-conjugate(A_n^+)/(2pi*i). In particular rho_0 has poles at +/-i. A proposed
further contour shift reaching those lines requires explicit residues.
No such pole is crossed in the preceding half-shift identity. There is no
hidden residue term available to erase the H_(-1) boundary feature.

7. What the straightforward 2x2 comparison does and does not prove

Cauchy-Schwarz bounds the off-diagonal pairing in section6 using both norms
with weight rho_(n+1). Replacing the first of those weights by rho_(n-1)
with a uniform scalar is impossible even at fixed positive geometry.
For fixed theta in (0,pi/2), the nearest upper t-plane pole yields, as T->infty,
  rho_n(-T) ~ [pi*eta^(2n)/(2*rho^(n+2)*(n!)^2*sin(2theta))]
                 *T^n*exp(-(pi-2theta)T).
This follows by the residue at t=i(pi-2theta), of order n+1; shifting to a
line between it and the next pole gives an exponentially smaller remainder.
Consequently, for n>=1,
  rho_(n+1)(-T)/rho_(n-1)(-T)
      ~ eta^4*T^2/(rho^2*n^2*(n+1)^2).
Thus a pointwise positive block with diagonal C*rho_(n-1),C*rho_(n+1) and
off-diagonal rho_(n+1) cannot hold with finite C. This excludes only that
unweighted adjacent-energy comparison. The original modes are linked, and
the charged dissipative density has an additional |omega| tail which may
control this loss. No counterexample to the full response follows.

8. The exact full-response operator inequality still to prove

For actual clock velocity rdot=delta+ell*r, delta>0, ell real, set
  kappa=-2delta-3ell.
At fixed labels the linked L_n do not move. Direct differentiation gives
  sdot=kappa/eta,
  rhodot_n=-4ell*rho_n
       -(2delta/R)*[(n+2)*a*rho_n+b*partial_theta rho_n].
The theta derivative here acts on the cosh/Gamma angular factor with eta and
rho fixed; their scale derivatives have already been included. Therefore
  E_Fdot=-4ell E_F
   +sum_n integral_{k,omega} {
       -(2delta/R)*[(n+2)*a*rho_n+b*rho_n,theta]*Q_n,s[L_n]
       +(kappa/eta)*rho_n*partial_s Q_n,s[L_n] }.
This is the full original linked response, up to the same constant 2pi and
external pi/4. It includes every quartic cross term. No chart remainder or
independent neutral jet is introduced.

The remaining desired state-independent estimate is precisely
  sum_n integral {
       -(2delta/R)*[(n+2)*a*rho_n+b*rho_n,theta]*Q_n,s[L_n]
       +(kappa/eta)*rho_n*partial_s Q_n,s[L_n] }
       <= C*fraction * sum_n integral rho_n*Q_n,s[L_n],
for the ACTUAL admissible clock parameters and ALL finite source families in
section1, with one C independent of labels, widths, polynomial degrees and
number of packets. The already controlled -4ell E term is added afterward.
All singular completed pieces must be evaluated as the combined raw forms.
The b=0 boundary must be obtained through their joint limit, not by treating
s and rho_n independently.

A potentially useful next comparison must retain the charged densities

not just the E0 densities. Their Mellin tails have the extra linear |omega|
scale that the failed adjacent-norm comparison loses. An estimate for those
shifted pairings against the actual summed charged form, with the exact
