A. Conventions, original class, and scope

Use the parent artifact's exact full Fourier/Mellin representation. Fix
alpha,beta>0, r=a+i b, 0<b<a, R=a^2+b^2, A=a^2-b^2,
eta=2b, p=alpha*r^2/2, rho=|p|, theta=arg p in (0,pi/2),
and the strict integrability floor alpha*A>8*beta*b^2.
All widths w_i>=1, coefficients, neutral complex sources and polynomials
are fixed finite original labels. Their Fourier amplitudes G_i(k) are
polynomials times Gaussians; all finite neutral derivative streams have
the same property. Labels are fixed under clock differentiation.

Set z_+=t/2+i theta, z_-=t/2-i theta and
  d_n=pi*eta^(2n)/(n!*(2rho)^(n+2)),
  C_n(t)=d_n*cosh(z_+)^(-(n+1))*cosh(z_-)^(-1),
  rho_n(omega)=(1/(2pi)) integral C_n(t) exp(-i omega t) dt,
  D_n(omega)=(n+2)*a*rho_n(omega)+b*partial_theta rho_n(omega).
The theta derivative holds eta and rho fixed. Every rho_n is nonnegative.
The kernel-to-density Fourier convention is fixed throughout this note.

For any original profile stream G define
  H_n[G](k,omega)=sum_i w_i^(-(n+2)/2+i omega)G_i(k),
  L_n[G](k,omega)=k^n H_n[G](k,omega), n>=0.
Also define H_-1[G]=sum_i w_i^(-1/2+i omega)G_i; no separate H_-1 norm is
asserted. The exact linkage is H_(n+1)(omega)=H_n(omega+i/2).
The completed quartic form Q_n,s from the parent artifact is NOT positive
for individual n. None of the inequalities below is multiplied by Q_n,s.

B. Exact charged-density lower bound, with the Fourier signs explicit

Define
  Astar=a-2b*cot(2theta)
       =(a^4+4a^2*b^2-b^4)/(2a(a^2-b^2)),
  Bstar=2b*csc(2theta)=R^2/(2a(a^2-b^2)).
Both are positive, and
  Astar-a/2=b^2(5a^2-b^2)/(2a(a^2-b^2))>0.
These identities use theta=2atan(b/a), not an independently chosen angle.

With the stated Fourier convention,
  FT(C_n')=i omega rho_n,
so the inverse kernel for omega*rho_n is -i C_n'. Since
  C_n'/C_n=-[(n+1)tanh(z_+)+tanh(z_-)]/2,
  (partial_theta C_n)/C_n=-i(n+1)tanh(z_+)+i tanh(z_-),
we obtain exactly
  D_n-2b omega rho_n
    =[a+(n+1)Astar]rho_n
      +(n+1)Bstar FT[d_n*cosh(z_+)^(-(n+2))],
  D_n+2b omega rho_n
    =[(n+1)a+Astar]rho_n
      +Bstar FT[d_n*cosh(z_+)^(-n)*cosh(z_-)^(-2)].
The elementary decomposition used here is
  a cosh(z_+)-2ib sinh(z_+)
       =Astar cosh(z_+)+Bstar cosh(z_-),
and its conjugate-sign counterpart.

Every residual Fourier density is nonnegative: a shifted inverse cosh
power has a positive Gamma Fourier density, and products give convolutions.
At n=0 the second residual is simply d_0*cosh(z_-)^(-2); there is no negative
power or undefined Gamma factor.
For omega>=0 use the first identity; for omega<=0 use the second. Hence
  D_n(omega) >= [(n+3)*a/2+2b*|omega|] rho_n(omega),  n>=0.       (B1)
This is exact at every frequency and every n, not just an asymptotic bound.
As a sign cross-check, the explicit rho_0 has
  partial_theta rho_0/rho_0 ~ 2omega-2cot(2theta) as omega->+infinity;
therefore D_0-2b omega rho_0 has only a bounded multiple of rho_0 there.
The first residual above, the pure-plus kernel, is exponentially smaller
in this positive-frequency tail, as required.

C. A uniform independent 2-by-2 comparison on theta<=pi/3

This section is useful for unlinked auxiliary pairings. The stronger linked
identity in sectionD does not need the additional theta<=pi/3 restriction.
Assume 0<theta<=pi/3. In the positive Gamma convolution for rho_m, the
conditional sigma density, up to its normalization, is
  f_m(sigma)=exp(-4theta*sigma)
      |Gamma((m+1)/2+i sigma)|^2 / cosh(pi*(omega-sigma)).
The exact Gamma recurrence, including BOTH factorial ratios, gives
  rho_(m+2)/rho_m
    =eta^4/[rho^2*(m+1)^2*(m+2)^2]
       *E[ ((m+1)/2)^2+sigma^2 ].                              (C1)

For x>0 the integer/half-integer Gamma product implies
  d/dx log|Gamma((m+1)/2+i x)|^2
       <= m/x-pi*tanh(pi*x).
For odd m, the elementary starting factor is x/sinh(pi*x); its log derivative
is 1/x-pi*coth(pi*x), which is even smaller than the stated bound. Thus m=0
and both parities are included.
Put M=|omega|+2(m+1). On both tails |sigma|>=M, the outward logarithmic
derivative of f_m is at most
  1/2+4pi/3-2pi*tanh(pi) <= 1/2-pi/2 <= -1.
Here pi>=3 and tanh(pi)>=11/12 suffice. The latter follows, for example,
from exp(2pi)>=exp(6)>=1+6+18=25 and (25-1)/(25+1)=12/13>=11/12.

For completeness, the normalized tail-moment argument is NOT inferred from
f(M+u) alone. The derivative bound gives
  f(M+u+v)<=exp(-u)f(M+v), u,v>=0.
Integrating over v yields S(u)<=exp(-u)S(0) for the unnormalized right-tail
survival S; the left tail satisfies the same inequality. Consequently each
tail's conditional excess has first moment<=1 and second moment<=2. Combining
both tails and the interior gives
  E sigma^2 <= M^2+2M+2 <= 2omega^2+32(m+1)^2.
Thus (C1) implies
  rho_(m+2) <= 33eta^4*((m+1)^2+omega^2)
                 /[rho^2*(m+1)^2*(m+2)^2] *rho_m.              (C2)

For n>=1, apply (C2) with m=n-1. By (B1) and a>b,
  D_(n-1)D_(n+1)
    >= (b^2/4)*(n^2+omega^2)*rho_(n-1)rho_(n+1).
Since eta^2=4b^2 this proves
  rho_(n+1) <= [8sqrt(33)b/(rho*n*(n+1))]
                  sqrt(D_(n-1)D_(n+1)).                      (C3)
Equivalently the Hermitian block with diagonal c_n D_(n-1),c_n D_(n+1)
and off-diagonal exp(i phi)rho_(n+1) is positive for every real phi, where
c_n=8sqrt(33)b/(rho*n*(n+1)). This repairs the failed energy-only tail
comparison in the parent artifact. It does not by itself prove the full
quartic response bound.

D. Exact polarized linked pairing: both real and imaginary parts

Define q_n=eta^2/[2rho(n+1)]. The two elementary Fourier shift identities are
  exp(i theta)rho_(n+1)(omega+i/2)
    +exp(-i theta)rho_(n+1)(omega-i/2)=2q_n rho_n(omega),
  exp(i theta)rho_(n+1)(omega+i/2)
    -exp(-i theta)rho_(n+1)(omega-i/2)
       =i*q_n/(n+1)*(partial_theta rho_n-2omega rho_n).          (D1)
To check the second sign directly,
  partial_theta C_n+2i C_n'=-2i(n+1)tanh(z_+)C_n,
so
  FT(tanh(z_+)C_n)=i/(2(n+1))*(partial_theta rho_n-2omega rho_n).
Also sinh(z_+)C_(n+1)=q_n tanh(z_+)C_n. These facts give (D1).

Let G,J be two finite profile streams with the SAME width list (zero padding
allows a union of finite lists). For n>=0 define the regular product
  I_n[G,J](k)=k^(2n) integral rho_(n+1)(omega)
       H_(n-1)[G](k,omega)*conjugate(H_(n+1)[J](k,omega)) domega.
At n=0 this contains H_-1 only as part of the displayed product. Do not
introduce a separately bounded negative-index norm, or k^(-1)L_-1.

Apply (D1) to H_n[G] H_n[J]^*, shift the two contours oppositely, and use the
actual width linkage. The result is the FULL complex sesquilinear identity
  exp(i theta) I_n[G,J](k)
    =q_n integral [rho_n+i*(partial_theta rho_n-2omega rho_n)/(2(n+1))]
          L_n[G](k,omega)*conjugate(L_n[J](k,omega)) domega.   (D2)
For G=J its real part is the parent artifact's identity. Its imaginary part
is new and is essential; a shifted function was never identified with its
shifted conjugate. For G different from J the two shifted terms are
exp(i theta)I_n[G,J] and exp(-i theta)conjugate(I_n[J,G]); summing and
subtracting them proves (D2) without any independent-mode assumption.

Contour justification: rho_(n+1) is holomorphic for
|Im omega|<(n+3)/2 and decays exponentially on every smaller closed horizontal
strip, with any rate less than pi-2theta. Finite width sums are bounded on
horizontal strips. The half shifts therefore cross no poles, and the
vertical rectangle sides tend to zero. This remains true at n=0. Nearest
rho_0 poles at +/-i do not enter (D2), whose shifted density is rho_1.
The H_-1 term is retained exactly, not discarded by a contour argument.

E. A full summed charged bound for the linked pair, including n=0

From (B1),
  rho_n <= 2D_n/[a(n+3)],
  |partial_theta rho_n-2omega rho_n| <= 4D_n/b.                (E1)
Indeed b partial_theta rho_n=D_n-(n+2)a rho_n, and the three terms
D_n, (n+2)a rho_n and 2b|omega|rho_n are bounded by D_n, 2D_n and D_n.
Using (D2), Cauchy-Schwarz with the POSITIVE weight D_n, and a>b gives
  (n+1)^2 |I_n[G,J](k)|
    <= (8b/rho)
       sqrt( integral D_n|L_n[G]|^2 * integral D_n|L_n[J]|^2 ). (E2)
For the constant, before simplification the coefficient is
  (n+1)^2*q_n*(2/[a(n+3)]+2/[b(n+1)])
     =eta^2/rho*((n+1)/(a(n+3))+1/b) <= 8b/rho.
Thus a second Cauchy-Schwarz, over n and k, yields the genuine full-sequence
bound
  sum_(n>=0) (n+1)^2 integral_k |I_n[G,J](k)| dk
    <= (8b/rho) sqrt( Dvac[G]*Dvac[J] ),
  Dvac[G]=sum_(n>=0) integral_(k,omega) D_n|L_n[G]|^2.         (E3)
For G=J, Dvac is exactly R/2 times the real charged dissipation of the vacuum
channel at fixed b,L. Formula (E3) uses the genuine whole linked sequence,
includes n=0, and has a constant independent of widths, number of packets,
neutral source labels and their polynomial degrees. The independent Gamma
comparison (C3) is unnecessary for (E3), but remains useful for auxiliary
pairings that do not obey the exact linkage.

All sums and k integrals are finite for the stated original packet streams:
the same Gaussian/factorial domination in the parent artifact applies, with
polynomial n factors and theta derivatives absorbed in a small neighborhood
of the strict packet floor. Finite neutral derivative streams are legitimate
choices of G,J. This does NOT mean that their Dvac norms are automatically
bounded by the full field-weighted charged dissipation.

F. What remains in the actual quartic conversion

The original full energy is sum_n integral rho_n Q_n,s[L_n], where
s=L/eta and Q=q0+q1/2+q2/4 is the exact completed degree-two field form in the
parent artifact. The charged form is sum_n integral D_n Q_n,s[L_n]. It is
nonnegative by the existing full fixed-v charged contraction, but individual
Q_n,s can be negative. Scalar (B1), (C3), or (E1) cannot be multiplied by Q.

The raw quartic moment formula is regular. In its completed form, the n=1
squares separately have k^(-2) singularities which cancel in their sum.
Estimating those squares individually would lose that cancellation. Likewise,
although (E3) applies to each actual neutral derivative stream, the resulting
norms Dvac[D^j G] are not yet bounded by the positive full charged field form:
large neutral translations can cancel the field shift inside its linked jets.
No such diagonal graph-norm comparison is being assumed.

A valid remaining step must apply (D2)/(E3) inside a positive full-feature
representation of the RAW j=0,1,2 moment Gram, or prove a joint norm comparison
that preserves these cancellations. The desired final inequality is still
  sum_n integral {
      -(2delta/R)*D_n Q_n,s[L_n]
      +(kappa/eta)*rho_n partial_s Q_n,s[L_n] }
      <= C*fraction *sum_n integral rho_n Q_n,s[L_n],
with the actual clock kappa=-2delta-3ell. The controlled -4ell energy term
is then restored. No bound for this full expression is claimed here.

At b->0 the factor 8b/rho in (E3) vanishes, but s=L/eta diverges and the
completed quartic coefficients contain compensating powers. Therefore one
must take the JOINT raw-channel limit; (E3) alone is not a uniform b=0 proof.
The n=0 PAIR boundary is now controlled by (D2)/(E3); that is a genuine
advance over assigning an uncontrolled H_-1 norm. The full k=0 quartic
cancellation and the conversion to the actual charged norm remain open.
