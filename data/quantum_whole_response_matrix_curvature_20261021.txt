1. Scope, original labels, and conventions

Keep exactly the original finite packet class and Fourier convention from
quantum_charged_mellin_pairing_20261021.txt, SHA256
b6b472c6af208ccdd67214c196016de5439e98457a014e5380b5182063755d8a.
Use a>0, |b|<a, alpha,beta>0, eta=2b, p=alpha*(a+ib)^2/2,
L=3-2a, nu=L-i*eta, and alpha*(a^2-b^2)>2*beta*eta^2.
The widths w_i>=1 are real and fixed under differentiation. Equal widths
may first be merged by summing their actual neutral Fourier profiles.
Let g_i(k) denote the resulting original Fourier streams, including d_i=c_iw_i.
They remain linked to Dg_i and D^2g_i, with D=i*d/dk.

For fixed k let A(k):C^N -> L^2(du dv dY), Y>0, have columns
  phi_i(k;u,v,Y)=exp(-w_i*(p*(u^2+v^2)+conj(p)*Y))*exp(-eta*k*v).
V denotes multiplication by the REAL coordinate v. Define Hermitian moments
  K_m=A^* V^m A, m=0,...,4; K=K_0.
These definitions retain every charged-width cross term. Matrix entries use
the standard convention (A^*A)_ij=integral conj(phi_i)*phi_j.
Then K_m'= -2eta*K_(m+1), wherever the next moment is used.

The Fourier-normalized physical field channels are exactly
  h_0=A g,
  h_1=A Dg+nu*V A g,
  h_2=A D^2g+2nu*V A Dg+nu^2*V^2 A g.
Indeed h_j=(D+Lv)^j[A(k)g(k)], because D A=-i eta V A.
Thus E_F=integral [||h_0||^2+||h_1||^2/2+||h_2||^2/4] dk.
The physical x-space energy is 2pi E_F; the unchanged model prefactor pi/4
can be restored to all forms simultaneously.

2. Exact finite-dimensional orthogonalization

For distinct widths the block Gram matrix of the columns of A,VA,V^2A is
strictly positive. To see this, an a.e. vanishing finite sum is continuous
and hence vanishes everywhere. At fixed v,Y, varying u^2 gives independent
exponentials exp(-p*w_i*u^2). A Vandermonde argument separates each width;
varying v then separates the coefficients of 1,v,v^2. Thus all coefficients
vanish. In particular the following inverses exist at every real k:

  M=K^-1 K_1,
  Sigma=K_2-K_1 K^-1 K_1 > 0,
  Q=K^-1 Sigma,
  J=Sigma^-1 (K_3-K_1 K^-1 K_2),
  Omega=K_4-K_2 K^-1 K_2
        -(K_3-K_2 K^-1 K_1) Sigma^-1 (K_3-K_1 K^-1 K_2) > 0.

Put N=VA-AM, and
  O=V^2A-A K^-1 K_2-NJ.
Then the three column spaces A,N,O are pairwise orthogonal and
  A^*A=K, N^*N=Sigma, O^*O=Omega.
This is exact matrix Gram orthogonalization; it does not select independent
angular modes, discard cross-width cancellation, or assume a uniform lower
bound on any individual inverse matrix.

3. Exact positive first and second channel forms

Define the actual k-differential connection
  T=D+nu M.
Because K'=-2eta K_1 and K_1'=-2eta K_2,
  M'=-2eta Q.
Therefore
  D^2g+2nu M Dg+nu^2 K^-1K_2 g
     =T^2g+|nu|^2 Qg.
The factor |nu|^2 is exact: nu^2+2i eta nu=|nu|^2.
Consequently, pointwise in k,

  ||h_1||^2 = ||Tg||_K^2 + |nu|^2 ||g||_Sigma^2,

  ||h_2||^2 = ||T^2g+|nu|^2Qg||_K^2
            + ||2nu Dg+nu^2 Jg||_Sigma^2
            + |nu|^4 ||g||_Omega^2.

Here ||z||_H^2=z^*Hz. All displayed terms are positive; the entire genuine
quartic norm has been retained. In particular the negative completed Mellin
Q_n forms have not been used as weights in a scalar density inequality.

There is also an intrinsic second connection. Let
  M_1=Sigma^-1 N^*VN, T_1=D+nu M_1.
Differentiating Sigma, or expanding N^*VN, gives
  J=M+M_1, Sigma'=-2eta Sigma M_1.
Thus the middle square is equivalently |nu|^2 ||(T+T_1)g||_Sigma^2.
Neither this expression nor the moment definitions require division by eta.
The connection T is formally symmetric in the varying metric K:
its formal adjoint equals T because K'=-2eta KM and nu=L-i eta.
No symmetry of the full second-order expression is needed for the identities.

4. Regular b=0 and k=0 limits, and integration

All definitions in sections 1--3 use ordinary finite moments and no 1/k or
1/b. At b=0, eta=0 and the v parity gives K_1=K_3=0, M=J=0,
Sigma=K_2, Omega=K_4-K_2 K^-1K_2. The formulas reduce to

  ||h_1||^2=||Dg||_K^2+L^2||g||_(K_2)^2,
  ||h_2||^2=||D^2g+L^2K^-1K_2g||_K^2
            +4L^2||Dg||_(K_2)^2+L^4||g||_Omega^2.

Here the K_m are independent of k. After the justified k integration by
parts, the second identity is also
  E_2=integral [||D^2g||_K^2+6L^2||Dg||_(K_2)^2
                         +L^4||g||_(K_4)^2] dk.
This is the exact common-width Hermite-type coefficient 6, but for the FULL
cross-width moment matrices; no uniform comparison of K_2 and K_4 follows
from it automatically.

The original strict Gaussian floor gives L1 for the raw channel norms and
for their actual a,L derivatives in a parameter neighborhood. Since each
positive term in the decomposition is bounded by the corresponding raw
channel norm pointwise, its k integral exists without separately estimating
large inverse matrices. Parameter derivatives should first be taken in the
raw original form; differentiating separate inverse-matrix terms without a
fresh domination argument would not be justified by this observation alone.

5. Charged dissipation as a second positive Gram form

At fixed b,L and fixed labels define H_m=-partial_a K_m. The known fixed-v
charged contraction says that, after u,Y integration, the matrix kernel
  -partial_a [integral conj(phi_i)*phi_j du dY]
is positive for each v,k. Consequently the full block moment matrix (H_(r+s))
for r,s=0,1,2 is positive semidefinite. This conclusion applies to arbitrary
vector polynomials of v, and hence to the genuine three linked channels.
It gives the total nonnegative charged form
  H_E=-partial_a E_F >=0.
No assumption that an independently chosen radial-polynomial angular block
contracts is made. That stronger relaxation fails in the separate enlarged-
block diagnostic, and is not needed for this positive fixed-v Gram statement.

The scalar Mellin result further implies the genuine zeroth-moment matrix
bound, for 0<b<a,
  H_0 >= (3a/(a^2+b^2))*K.
Indeed H_0 has the exact spectral form (2/(a^2+b^2))*sum_n D_n|L_n|^2,
and D_n>=((n+3)a/2+2b|omega|)rho_n. The b=0 case also follows directly
from H_0=(4/a)K. This is a useful zeroth-channel coercivity statement, but
it is NOT automatically a coercivity estimate on the block of all moments.
Differentiating a positive matrix inequality in k does not preserve its sign.

6. Fully regular finite matrix statement of the remaining comparison

For any Hermitian moment sequence Z=(Z_0,...,Z_4), write B(Z,nu) for the
Hermitian 3-by-3 block matrix, in the jet order (g,Dg,D^2g), with lower
triangular entries

  B00=Z0+|nu|^2 Z2/2+|nu|^4 Z4/4,
  B10=nu*(Z1+|nu|^2 Z3)/2,
  B20=nu^2 Z2/4,
  B11=Z0/2+|nu|^2 Z2,
  B21=nu Z1/2,
  B22=Z0/4.

The upper entries are Hermitian adjoints. Then, exactly,
  E_F=integral jet(g)^* B(K,nu) jet(g) dk,
  H_E=integral jet(g)^* B(H,nu) jet(g) dk,
  partial_L E_F=integral jet(g)^* partial_L B(K,nu) jet(g) dk,
where K_m are held fixed in the last derivative and dnu/dL=1.
The required whole physical response estimate, apart from the separately
controlled -4ell E_F term, is precisely

  integral jet(g)^* [kappa*partial_L B(K,nu)-delta*B(H,nu)] jet(g) dk
       <= C*fraction * integral jet(g)^* B(K,nu) jet(g) dk.

It is required only for the actual original finite profiles and their linked
first/second k derivatives. A pointwise-in-k block Loewner inequality would
be a sufficient stronger result, but it has NOT been proved here and cannot
be assumed by treating the jets as independent. The exact decomposition
above permits investigation through the positive matrices Sigma,Omega and
the metric connections, retaining the missing neutral uncertainty. Neither
H_0>=cK nor the scalar polarized Mellin pairing estimate alone supplies the
needed comparison between those connections/curvatures for K and H.
