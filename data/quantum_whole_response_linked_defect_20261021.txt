1. Precisely retained original class and physical parameters

Use alpha,beta>0, a>0, r=a+ib, |b|<a, eta=2b, L=3-2a,
p=alpha*r^2/2, nu=L-i*eta, and the strict floor
  alpha*(a^2-b^2)>2*beta*eta^2.
The intended uniform target is on the ACTUAL physical clock range, including
b=0, with the original physical width hypotheses (in particular alpha>=32beta).
It is not asserted on every abstract parameter tuple satisfying only the
strict inequality above.

For a finite original family keep every label fixed under geometry/clock
variation:
  w_i>=1 real, d_i=c_i*w_i,
  f_i(zeta)=d_i*exp(-beta*zeta^2+B_i*zeta)*P_i(zeta).
Let g_i(k)=(1/(2pi))*integral exp(-ikx) f_i(x) dx, so
  f_i(x)=integral exp(ikx) g_i(k) dk,  D=i*d/dk.
All neutral sources B_i, polynomial coefficients, widths, and amplitudes
are fixed. Equal charged widths can first be merged by summing their actual
g_i; this does not make any of their derivatives independent.

All norms and inner products below use
  dmu=dk du dv dY,  k,u,v real, Y>0.
Define the exact original Fourier feature and linked field channels
  phi_i=exp(-w_i*(p*(u^2+v^2)+conj(p)*Y))*exp(-eta*k*v),
  psi=sum_i phi_i*g_i,
  X=D+L*v,
  h_j=X^j psi  (j=0,1,2).
Equivalently
  h_j=sum_i phi_i*(D+nu*v)^j g_i.
The entire quartic energy, in Fourier normalization, is
  E_F=sum_(j=0)^2 c_j*||h_j||^2,  (c_0,c_1,c_2)=(1,1/2,1/4).
The physical x-space integral is 2pi*E_F; restoring the original pi/4 model
prefactor multiplies every displayed energy and response by the same factor.
No angular mode, charged-width cross term, or neutral derivative is dropped.

2. Finite-label operator notation; no global domain claim

On this finite-feature module let W multiply the label w_i:
  W(sum_i phi_i a_i(k,v))=sum_i w_i phi_i a_i(k,v).
The coefficients generated here are polynomials in v with genuine derivatives
of the original g_i. After merging equal widths, W is unambiguous: at fixed
v,Y,k, varying u^2 separates the distinct exponentials exp(-p*w_i*u^2).
This notation asserts neither that W is selfadjoint in the physical norm nor
that it extends to a closed or accretive operator on an arbitrary completion.
Such assertions would need a separate proof.

Put
  Theta=alpha*b^2*(u^2+v^2+Y)*W
        -i*alpha*a*b*(u^2+v^2-Y)*W-eta*k*v.
The last term is ordinary scalar multiplication. Direct differentiation at
fixed a,L and fixed labels gives EXACTLY
  b*partial_b psi=Theta psi,
  [X,Theta]=-i*eta*v,
  b*partial_b h_j=Theta h_j-i*j*eta*v*h_(j-1),
  partial_L h_j=j*v*h_(j-1).
For j=0 the adjacent term is absent, so no h_(-1) has to be defined.
In particular the sign of the charged phase is negative:
  -w_i*b*partial_b p=alpha*w_i*b^2-i*alpha*w_i*a*b.

4. Minimal genuinely linked operator inequality

The exact raw form of J is

  J=2 Re sum_(j=0)^2 c_j integral conj(h_j)
        *[Theta h_j+j*(3chi-i*eta)*v*h_(j-1)] dmu.              (A)

For each fixed u,v,Y and j=1,2, the integral
  integral conj(h_j)*h_(j-1) dk
is real: h_j=X h_(j-1), X=i*d/dk+Lv is formally symmetric, and the boundary
term vanishes by the Gaussian estimates in section 6. Multiplication by the
real, k-independent v preserves this conclusion. Therefore the imaginary
adjacent part in (A) integrates to zero, giving equivalently

  J=2 Re sum_j c_j integral conj(h_j)*Theta h_j dmu
       +6chi sum_(j=1)^2 j*c_j Re integral conj(h_j)*v*h_(j-1) dmu.   (B)

This cancellation uses the genuine linked h_j=X^j psi. It is not an entrywise
matrix cancellation at fixed k and does not hold for three independent jets.
It is also the same cancellation obtained from the physical Plancherel
identity for (x+Lv)^(2j-1)|Phi|^2.

The precise OPEN inequality is

  J >= -C*E_F,                                                (C)

uniformly for the actual physical clock and all original finite families,
with C depending at most on the fixed physical model parameters, not on the
number of labels, their widths, amplitudes, sources, polynomial degrees, or
clock position. A sufficient theorem may specify its dependence on alpha,
beta and the prescribed physical parameter range explicitly.

If (C) is proved with C>=0, the existing clock bounds imply
  Edot_F <= (16+20C)*t*E_F.
The 16 is exactly 4*(8*b^2)<=16. Thus (C) really is sufficient for the
unrestricted physical response; it is not a surrogate independent-block
positivity problem. Conversely (A) and the clock identity express the actual
remaining form exactly. No bound (C), and no counterexample to it, is proved
by this handoff.

6. Concrete regularity sufficient for all identities above

There are no new unproved regularity hypotheses for an original finite family.
Each g_i and each of its k derivatives is a polynomial times
  exp((B_i-ik)^2/(4beta)),
up to a fixed scalar. Hence any finite collection of derivatives has a bound
  C*(1+|k|)^m*exp(-k^2/(4beta)+C*|k|).
For a single feature, its squared modulus has the quadratic exponent
  -alpha*w_i*(a^2-b^2)*(u^2+v^2+Y)-k^2/(2beta)-2eta*k*v
plus at most a linear k term and a polynomial factor. Complete the square:
  -k^2/(2beta)-2eta*k*v
    =-(k+2beta*eta*v)^2/(2beta)+2beta*eta^2*v^2.
The strict floor and w_i>=1 leave a positive coefficient for v^2; u^2 and Y
already have positive coefficients. Linear k and every fixed polynomial in
k,u,v,Y can be absorbed into a slightly weaker Gaussian/Laplace majorant.
For a fixed finite family the factors w_i and any of their finite powers are
just constants. The same argument works in a small compact parameter
neighborhood with positive floor margin.

Consequently all h_j, Theta h_j, v*h_j and all finitely many k/geometry
 derivatives used above are L2; all displayed products are L1 by Cauchy--Schwarz.
Fubini, geometry differentiation under the integral, and the k integration
by parts in section 4 are justified. At fixed u,v,Y, all needed k boundary
terms decay Gaussianly. This proof gives packet-dependent domination, which
is enough for the exact identities; it is NOT a state-independent bound on
those dominating constants and does not prove (C).

For future Lean integration, a natural next formal interface is the actual
original feature family together with its derived L2 first/second field
channels, their derived geometry derivatives, and the exact equality (A).
The target theorem must retain h_j=X^j psi and the fixed-label derivative
convention. An abstract theorem that assumes (C), assumes the required
numerical-range bound, or supplies independent jets would not close the gap.
