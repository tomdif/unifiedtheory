EXACT FAILURE OF WIDTH-FLOOR v^2 COERCIVITY IN THE ORIGINAL FINITE CLASS
2026-10-21. Analytic successor, NOT Lean verified.
No repository source, frozen evidence, or previous research note changed.

1. Scope

This note rules out a specific stronger intermediate estimate:
  -partial_a E >= c*alpha*a*integral v^2 W(x+Lv)|Phi|^2
with c>0 independent of the original finite charged-width family.
All neutral jets and the full quartic W are retained. The charged-width
floor w_i>=1 does NOT imply this estimate. This is not a counterexample to
the actual clock response, whose geometric/dilation cancellations are kept
separate below. In particular no failure of the target uniform response
bound is inferred from failure of this stronger sufficient condition.

2. Original geometry and an exact confluent limit

Fix b=0, a>0, alpha,beta>0, w0>=1, L real. Put p=alpha*a^2/2 and
  z=p*(u^2+v^2+Y), Y>0,
  Phi_N(z,x)=z^N exp(-w0*z)*exp(-beta*x^2), N>=0.
This is the Nth charged-width confluence of original finite packets. The
neutral source is zero and its polynomial is 1. It preserves the original
neutral width beta and can satisfy alpha>=32beta. All labels of each
finite approximant are held fixed under the a derivative.

Let W(s)=1+s^2/2+s^4/4. Integrating only x gives
  integral W(x+Lv)exp(-2beta*x^2) dx = c0+c2*v^2+c4*v^4,
where, writing M_j=integral x^j exp(-2beta*x^2) dx,
  c0=M0+M2/2+M4/4 > 0,
  c2=L^2*(M0/2+3M2/2) >= 0,
  c4=L^4*M0/4 >= 0.
Thus this reduction is exactly the FULL original quartic norm, not a
replacement by its highest channel. The coefficients are independent of a
when the partial derivative holds b,L fixed.

3. Exact charged moments, including all cross terms

Define
  I_m(a)=integral_(u,v real,Y>0)
         v^(2m) z^(2N) exp(-2w0*z) du dv dY.
Put C_m=(2m choose m)/4^m. Polar coordinates in (u,v), followed by
X=u^2+v^2 and T=X+Y, give

  I_m=pi*C_m/(m+1) * p^(-m-2)
        *Gamma(2N+m+2)/(2w0)^(2N+m+2).

The angular average of v^(2m) is C_m*X^m, and
integral_0^T X^m dX=T^(m+1)/(m+1), which fixes all constants.
The exact moment ratio is

  I_(m+1)/I_m
    =(2m+1)*(2N+m+2)/[4*(m+2)*w0*p].

For m=0,1,2 it is at least (N+1)/(4*w0*p).
The full energy and v^2-weighted energy are
  E_N=c0*I0+c2*I1+c4*I2,
  V_N=c0*I1+c2*I2+c4*I3.
Consequently
  V_N/E_N >= (N+1)/(4*w0*p).
Since p_a/p=2/a and the coefficients are held fixed,
  -partial_a I_m=(2m+4)*I_m/a,
  4/a <= (-partial_a E_N)/E_N <= 8/a.
Therefore

  (-partial_a E_N)/(alpha*a*V_N)
      <= 16*w0/(N+1) -> 0.

The common model normalization and the physical Fourier normalization, if
included, cancel in this quotient. No independent angular or neutral jets
were chosen in this calculation.

4. Transfer to ACTUAL finite real-width families

For fixed N, tau>0, define

  F_(N,tau)(z)=exp(-w0*z)*[(1-exp(-tau*z))/tau]^N
       =sum_(j=0)^N d_j exp(-(w0+j*tau)*z),
  d_j=(-1)^j*(N choose j)/tau^N.

Every width w_j=w0+j*tau is real and >=1, and the original model amplitudes
are c_j=d_j/w_j because its charged coefficient is c_j*w_j. Every packet
has the same zero neutral source and P=1. The cross terms stay inside the
squared sum; they are not discarded or replaced by a diagonal sum.

As tau decreases to zero, F_(N,tau)->z^N exp(-w0*z). For real z>=0,
  |(1-exp(-tau*z))/tau| <= z,
  |d/dz [(1-exp(-tau*z))/tau]| <= 1.
Hence the functions and their first charged derivatives are bounded by a
fixed polynomial in z times exp(-w0*z), for each fixed N. The extra factors
v^2 W(x+Lv) and z_a=2z/a are polynomial. Gaussian/gamma integration therefore
proves convergence of E, V, and -partial_a E by dominated convergence.
These are also instances of the already frozen original-family confluence
route, but the elementary domination here is sufficient for this example.

Given any proposed c>0, first choose fixed N so that 16*w0/(N+1)<c.
Then choose tau>0 small enough that the strict inequality survives all
three convergences. This supplies an actual finite real-width family
violating the proposed v^2 cooling estimate. No uniform-in-N confluence
interchange is needed. All finite labels are frozen during differentiation.

This transfer concerns the stronger cooling comparison, not a divergent
actual clock response. It does not use the nonadmissible complex exponent
from the earlier independent radial-block diagnostic.

5. Fixed-v version and the precise flaw in common-width extraction

For any fixed holomorphic charged function F at b=0, put q=p*v^2. The exact
u,Y marginal is
  I_v(a)=2*p^(-3/2)*integral_q^infinity (z-q)^(1/2)|F(z)|^2 dz.
Thus, whenever the displayed finite integrals and differentiation apply,

  -partial_a I_v/I_v
    =3/a+(q/a)*[integral_q^infinity (z-q)^(-1/2)|F(z)|^2 dz]
                 /[integral_q^infinity (z-q)^(1/2)|F(z)|^2 dz].

For the fixed-label choice F(z)=(z-q0)^N exp(-w0*z), evaluated at q=q0,
this ratio is exactly 3/a+4*w0*q0/[a*(4N+1)]. At q0=N/w0 it is less than
4/a although v^2=N/(w0*p). This is another direct obstruction to a uniform
fixed-v v^2 lower bound. The full-quartic argument above already provides
the simpler transfer from actual confluence, so no additional transfer is
needed for this fixed-v diagnostic.

Factoring exp(-p*v^2) out of a charged-width sum does produce a scalar
negative derivative proportional to v^2. The remaining atoms, however,
have widths shifted only in the v^2 coefficient, while their u,Y widths
are unchanged. Their derivative need not satisfy the former contraction.
The exact example proves that assuming this preservation would be false.
Factoring the whole common charged exponential instead alters the u,Y
measure by parameter-dependent weights; the unweighted contraction does
not automatically transport to that weighted measure either.

6. Why this is not a physical response counterexample

The actual zero-imaginary clock geometry has a=1,b=0,r=1 and rdot=0.
The original physical response is therefore exactly zero for every fixed
finite family. In the corrected split one has delta=t, ell=-t, kappa=t,
L=1. The exact real charged scaling identity at eta=0 is

  a*partial_a E+L*partial_L E=-4E.

It holds for every fixed original profile, including each approximant.
Hence at a=L=1,
  -4ell E+delta*partial_a E+kappa*partial_L E=0.
No positive response is generated by the examples above. For each fixed
finite approximant, the failed strict cooling comparison also persists in
a sufficiently small b neighborhood by the original Gaussian domination,
but that continuity statement still does not break the combined response
identity or prove that its quotient diverges.
